** All RadarTypes definitions should contain the following properties:
**
**  Name (String)
**
**	The user friendly name of the RadarType (what shows up in DEdit).

**  DrawOrder (Int)
**
**	The relative order in which this item should be drawn. Higher numbered items are drawn on top of
**		lower numbered ones. These numnbers are completely arbitrary.
**
