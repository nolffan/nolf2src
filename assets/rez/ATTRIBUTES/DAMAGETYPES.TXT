/************************************************************************
**
** DamageTypes
**
**	These records define the attributes of the various damage types in the game
**
**	JarCamera (integer)
**		
**		Does this damage type cause the camera to be "jarred"
**		(0 = false, 1 = true)
**
**	Gadget (integer)
**		
**		Is this type a gadget damage type?
**		(0 = false, 1 = true)
**
**	Accuracy (integer)
**		
**		Does this damage type count towards player accuracy?
**		(0 = false, 1 = true)
**
**	SlowMovement (float)
**		
**		How long (in seconds) does this type of damage slow the player?
**		This is unrelated to the ForceWalk value
**		(0.0 for no slow down)
**
**	ScoreTag (integer)
**		
**		Does this damage type award a tag in multiplayer
**		(0 = false, 1 = true)
**
**	Grief (integer)
**		
**		Should a player be protected from multiple instances of this type in multiplayer
**		(0 = false, 1 = true)
**
**	HurtAnim (integer)
**		
**		Does this damage type cause the player to play a hurt animation if standing still.
**		(0 = false (default), 1 = true)
**
**	PlayerDamageSound0-N
**		
**		Sound file to use for player damage sounds.  Default:  pain01.wav - pain05.wav.
**
**
*/
